# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis 

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a Superstore sales dataset using **Microsoft Excel Online**.  
The objective is to uncover key business insights related to **sales performance, profitability, categories, regions, and customer demand**.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel Online
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Conditional Formatting
- Excel Dashboards

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: Superstore Dataset (Kaggle)
- Records include:
  - Order details
  - Product categories & sub-categories
  - Sales, profit, and discount
  - Region and state information

---

## 📈 Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** ₹ 2.29M  
- **Total Profit:** ₹ 286K  
- **Total Orders:** High order volume indicating strong demand  
- **Average Discount:** Identified impact on profitability  

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1️⃣ Overall Performance Analysis
- Calculated total sales, profit, orders, and average discount.
- Converted large values into readable formats (K, M).

### 2️⃣ Category-Level Analysis
- Compared **Sales vs Profit** across product categories.
- Identified high-sales but low-profit categories (risk areas).

### 3️⃣ Region & State Analysis
- Analyzed sales and profit by region and state.
- Used **conditional formatting** to highlight:
  - High-performing regions
  - Loss-making states

### 4️⃣ Time-Based Trends
- Monthly and yearly sales trends were analyzed to identify seasonality.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for quick insights
- Pivot charts for category comparison
- Heatmaps for regional performance
- Clean, executive-friendly layout

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High sales do not always translate to high profit.
- Technology category is the most profitable.
- Certain regions show strong sales but weak profitability due to heavy discounting.
- Discount strategy has a significant impact on margins.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations
- Optimize discount strategies in loss-making regions.
- Focus on high-profit categories and regions.
- Improve pricing and logistics efficiency.
